Help

Provider workflow guide

Use the portal to publish farm support services and manage real farmer requests. Payment support is not connected yet.

Listings

Create listings for rentals, transport, storage, inputs, consultation or repair. Use quote-required pricing when details must be reviewed first.

Requests

Open each farmer request, review location and dates, then quote or decline. Accepted quotes can be marked complete after work finishes.

Pending platform features

Payment support is not connected yet. Verification is pending platform review. Ratings, reviews and media uploads are coming soon.
